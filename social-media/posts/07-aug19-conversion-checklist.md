## Caption

A website that looks good but doesn't convert is just an expensive business card. 💳

Here are the 5 things every high-converting site needs — save this one. 📌

1️⃣ **A clear headline** — visitors should know what you do in 3 seconds.
2️⃣ **One obvious call-to-action** — "Call Now," "Book Online," "Get a Quote." Don't make them guess.
3️⃣ **Proof** — reviews, before/afters, real photos. Trust closes deals.
4️⃣ **Fast + mobile-friendly** — most of your traffic is on a phone.
5️⃣ **Easy contact** — click-to-call, a short form, no hoops.

Check your own site against this list. Missing 2 or more? That's leads walking out the door.

Save this, then send it to a business owner who needs it. 🔁
