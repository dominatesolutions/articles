# How to schedule all 30 days (post every 3 days automatically)

**Short answer: yes.** You can queue all 10 posts once and have Instagram + Facebook publish them for you every 3 days — no daily logging in. Here are your options, easiest first.

---

## Option 1 — Meta Business Suite (FREE, recommended)

This is Meta's own tool and it publishes to **both** Instagram and Facebook from one place. No third-party cost.

1. Go to **business.facebook.com** (or the Meta Business Suite app) and connect your Facebook Page + Instagram account.
2. Click **Create Post** → write once, and toggle **both** Facebook and Instagram on so it cross-posts.
3. Paste the caption from the matching file in `posts/`, upload the graphic from `graphics/`.
4. Instead of "Publish," click the dropdown → **Schedule** → set the date/time.
5. Repeat for all 10 posts using the dates below.

**Your schedule (11 AM ET is a good default):**

| # | Date | # | Date |
|---|------|---|------|
| 1 | Sat Aug 1 | 6 | Sun Aug 16 |
| 2 | Tue Aug 4 | 7 | Wed Aug 19 |
| 3 | Fri Aug 7 | 8 | Sat Aug 22 |
| 4 | Mon Aug 10 | 9 | Tue Aug 25 |
| 5 | Thu Aug 13 | 10 | Fri Aug 28 |

Meta Business Suite lets you schedule posts up to ~75 days out, so all 10 fit in one sitting.

---

## Option 2 — A social scheduler (Buffer, Later, Metricool, Hootsuite)

If you want a nicer calendar view, drag-and-drop, and analytics, use a dedicated tool. Most have a free or low-cost tier that covers 1 Instagram + 1 Facebook profile.

- **Buffer** — set up a posting "queue" with a repeating slot every 3 days, then drop the 10 posts in and it spaces them automatically.
- **Later** — visual calendar, great for Instagram; connect both accounts and schedule each post.
- **Metricool** — strong free tier, good for IG + FB together.

The "every 3 days" rhythm is easiest here: you define the recurring time slots once, and the tool fills them in order.

---

## Option 3 — Fully automated (advanced)

If you ever want this to run hands-off from a spreadsheet or these files, the **Meta Graph API** (or a tool like Make.com / Zapier + a Google Sheet) can publish on a schedule. Overkill for 10 posts — but doable if you want to scale to always-on content later.

---

## Recommended workflow

1. Generate/approve the 10 graphics (in `graphics/`).
2. Open **Meta Business Suite** (Option 1).
3. Schedule all 10 at once using the date table above.
4. Done — you're set for the full 30 days in about 20 minutes.

> Tip: after this batch, keep the same every-3-days rhythm going. Consistency beats volume — 10 solid posts a month, every month, will do more for your local visibility than a burst that fizzles out.
